PASSDAT-02Reloading restores habits, planner, journal, budget and payment plans from storage.
PASSDAT-03Export file: Data menu downloads a .json file containing all data.
PASSDAT-04Import file: choosing a valid export replaces the current data.

PASSDAT-07Settings persist across reloads: habit view, sort order, week start day, exchange-rate currencies, Pomodoro lengths, budget period.

Budget tracker

PASSBUD-01Budget layout: summary tiles above the period tabs; tabs are Week, Month, Year (default Month).
PASSBUD-02Each period has its own visualisation (week, month, year charts differ) and the summary figures follow the period.
PASSBUD-03Summary maths: income, expense, savings and left are correct for a known set of entries.
PASSBUD-04Summary tiles are not rebuilt or animated when a filter / same tab is chosen, never animate on period change, and do update when an entry is added.
PASSBUD-05Entries: amount, category with colour, note, tags, date; the entry shows in the list and the header shows "Spent £x this month".
PASSBUD-06Generic categories with distinct colours include Rent, Salary, Savings, Investments, Groceries, Bills, Transport etc., grouped into income / savings / expense.
PASSBUD-07Default currency is GBP; other currencies (incl. AED) are converted using the live rate, and the rate used is stored with the entry.
PASSBUD-08Repeat options: Once, Weekly, Selected days, Every 4 weeks, Monthly, Yearly (+ Until date).
PASSBUD-09Selected days: choose any combination of weekdays (e.g. Tue + Thu); occurrences fall only on those days, starting from the entry date.

PASSBUD-12Payment plan: full amount, APR, monthly payment and day; the schedule is generated until paid off; maths matches the standard amortisation formula.
PASSBUD-13Extra (early) payments can be added to a plan and shorten it.
PASSBUD-14Payment plans appear under "Recurring and payment plans" with progress and next payment.
PASSBUD-15Filter popover: Show (all/spent/income/saved), Include (all/recurring/one-off), Break down by (category/tag); each changes the numbers shown.
PASSBUD-16Breakdown shows every category / tag with its total amount and percentage of the whole.
PASSBUD-17Tags on budget entries: chips filter the entries; the tag field suggests tags used anywhere in the app.
PASSBUD-18Budget export button (CSV) is on the budget page.
